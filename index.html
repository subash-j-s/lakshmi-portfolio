About me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Meenakshi College of Engineering, Chennai.

My academic interests span emerging areas of computer science, with a focus on research that connects computing methods with practical applications. I have participated in faculty development programmes, including programmes focused on Artificial Intelligence, and continue to engage with professional learning and academic communities.
